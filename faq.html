How far in advance do I need to book catering?

All catering orders must be booked at least 7 days prior to the event.

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit is required upon confirmation of your catering order in order to secure your event date.

The remaining balance is due 3 days prior to the event.

Are deposits refundable?

No. All deposits are non-refundable.

What happens if I miss my final payment deadline?

A $100 cancellation fee will be applied in order to proceed with the booking.

If the cancellation fee is not paid within 24 hours of non-payment, the booking will automatically be cancelled.

What does the setup service include?

Setup is $165 per 5-count setup package.

Each package includes 5 racks, 5 water pans, 10 burners, and serving utensils.

Additional serving items require an additional setup fee.

What does the serving service include?

Serving is $150 per hour with a 3-hour minimum.

The service includes 3 servers, serving, cleanup, and breakdown of the catering order only.

How does delivery work?

Delivery is $125 per 7 pans.

Delivery includes transportation of food from the restaurant to the venue.

When the driver arrives, the client must meet the driver at the delivery vehicle to receive the items.

Food4Thought does not enter the venue unless setup or serving services have also been purchased.

Deliveries containing juices or liquids have an additional $35 fee.

How many people does a full steam pan feed?

All full steam pans feed up to 50 people when served properly.

How far in advance can I book a wedding?

Weddings must be booked within a 6-month window or further out.

For weddings booked within a 6-month window, a 50% deposit is required upon booking. The remaining balance is due 30 days prior to the event.

Can I book my wedding a year in advance?

Yes. For bookings made one year out, the deposit can be divided into four payments due quarterly, every 3 months, until the booking is paid off.

The final payment is due 30 days prior to the event.

Can I build my own wedding menu?

Yes! Food4Thought offers a Build Your Own Menu option.

The menu includes 1 protein, 2 sides, 1 salad, and rolls.

How much is a wedding consultation?

Wedding consultations are $75 per 30 minutes.

If you decide to book with Food4Thought, the consultation fee will be applied toward your balance.

What happens if I need to cancel or change my wedding date?

If you cancel your event or change the date, your deposit remains valid for 90 days from the initial booking date.

If you do not book within that 90-day timeframe, the deposit will not be redeemable.
