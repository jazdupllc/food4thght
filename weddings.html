WEDDING BOOKING

Planning
ahead matters.

01

Booking Window

Weddings must be booked within a 6-month window or further out.

02

Within 6 Months

A 50% deposit is required upon booking. The remaining balance is due 30 days prior to the event.

03

Booking 1 Year Out

The deposit will be divided into four payments due quarterly, every 3 months, until the booking is paid off.

Final payment is due 30 days prior to the event.

04

Non-Refundable Deposits

All deposits are non-refundable.

CANCELLATION POLICY

Important
information.

If the client chooses to cancel the event or change the date, deposits are valid for 90 days from the initial booking date.

If the client does not book within that 90-day timeframe, the deposit will not be redeemable.

If a client is late with a payment, an additional $100 cancellation fee will be added to the balance.

If the final balance is not paid within 24 hours of being late, the booking will be cancelled.
