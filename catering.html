BEFORE YOU BOOK

Let's get
the details right.

All catering orders must be booked at least 7 days prior to the event.

A 50% deposit is due upon confirmation of your order to secure your event date. The remaining balance is due 3 days prior to the event.

‼

NON-REFUNDABLE DEPOSITS

All deposits are non-refundable.

If the remaining balance is not paid by the due date, a $100 cancellation fee will be applied in order to proceed with the booking.

If the cancellation fee is not paid within 24 hours of non-payment, the booking will be automatically cancelled.
